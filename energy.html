Fero Energy · Battery swapping for delivery fleets

A delivery rider can't stop to charge. So we're building a way to move the energy instead.

Fero Energy is building an open, automated battery-swapping network for India's delivery fleets — a charged pack in two minutes, at a station the rider can see in the app before they ride. Our first stations are planned for Gurgaon.

The rider's problem

A rider paid per order works three peaks a day. A battery that needs hours to charge costs them one.

Breakfast, lunch and dinner — the money is in the rushes. Charging in the middle of the day means a short shift and a missed peak. What the rider needs is energy in two minutes, close by, at any hour.

2 minutes a swap

Drop the empty pack, take a full one, ride on.

A station nearby

Within about 1.5 km of where the orders are.

Open at 1 AM too

Unmanned, 24×7 — the late-night rush included.

Visible before they ride

Live status in the fleet's app, so nobody has to ride to an empty station.

What we're building

Open, automated, predictable — by design.

Automated

Stations will be unmanned and open 24×7. Packs charge inside the cabinet; the rider drops a flat one and takes a full one in two minutes.

Open

One pack spec — tray, connector and protocol — that we'll publish to every OEM. Any fleet, any scooter, one network.

Predictable

Live status in the fleet's app: open, packs ready, next pack in N minutes. Riders will reserve a pack before they ride, and uptime will be written into the contract.

For fleet operators

We'll sell to fleets, not riders.

The plan: one monthly price per scooter, the same price and API for every fleet — and a network sited where your riders actually ride.

Uptime in the contract

A service level we'll sign, not just a promise. Each station is planned to open with contracted riders, so it isn't left half-empty.

Sited where the rides are

Planned around dark-store clusters and delivery hubs, close together, placed on fleet ride data — not wherever someone owns a plot.

Pack health on every swap

Every charge cycle will be logged, so worn packs come off the road instead of going back to your riders.

A fleet API and console

Planned: station status, swaps and pack data for your operations team, through one API.

The riders are going electric faster than the swap stations are being built. Fero Energy will be an open, automated battery-swapping network for delivery fleets. No stations are live yet: the first are planned for Gurgaon, with Fero Mobility as the first customer. We're raising our seed round now.

The model

Utilisation is the north star

Swaps per slot per day will drive everything, so every station is planned around riders contracted to use it before it is built.

An open spec, not a closed garden

We'll own the pack spec, firmware, station software and fleet API, and buy cells and cabinets from vendors.

Contracts, not walk-ins

Fleets will sign monthly per-scooter contracts with uptime commitments; riders won't pay at the station.

City by city

A city opens only after the previous one works, on the same playbook and a local team.
