About

Two operators who'd already run this — now electrifying the whole last mile.

Fero was founded by two operators who ran rider operations, fleet operations, wallet health and collections for a multi-city electric two-wheeler delivery fleet. We saw, from the inside, where a rider's day actually breaks — a swap station that's out of charge, a repair that eats a shift, a wallet that goes negative without warning.

Fero Motion Technologies is that experience, rebuilt as two businesses. Fero Mobility rents electric scooters to delivery riders on one daily rent. Fero Energy is building the open battery-swapping network that keeps delivery fleets moving.

Our plan: to become India's largest EV rental player — and to give the last mile the energy network it needs.
