The model

India's gig workforce runs on two-wheelers it cannot afford to own, service, or replace. Fero takes the asset, the energy and the downtime off the rider and prices the whole thing as one weekly number — battery-less scooters on a swappable-battery network, so the fleet carries no battery capex or degradation risk.

Utilisation-led revenue

High daily fleet utilisation is the operating target that drives every pricing and centre-density decision.

Wallet health as a margin lever

Share of active riders with a positive wallet balance is tracked as a core metric, not an afterthought.

Fair-wear parts economics

Usage-based part pricing reduces disputes and churn while keeping maintenance margin honest.

OEM ambition

Long-term plan to move upstream into vehicle manufacturing for structural cost efficiency.

Roadmap

Single-city launch, proving centre density and utilisation before expanding.

Phase 2 — Core metro replication

Delhi-NCR, Bangalore, Hyderabad, Mumbai, Pune — same centre-density playbook, city by city.

Phase 3 — Beyond mobility

Use the rider relationship as a platform for the other things gig workers need — housing and financial services among them.
