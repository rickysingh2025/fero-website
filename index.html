Straight answers

Do I need a down payment or loan?

No. One week's rent plus a refundable deposit. No financing, no EMI, no ownership risk.

What if my bike breaks down mid-shift?

Bring it to your centre or call for recovery. Repairs are on us, and you leave on a replacement bike the same day.

Is the battery swap really free?

Included in your weekly rent. (Exact swap policy to be confirmed before launch.)

Can I use it for Zomato, Swiggy, Blinkit or Zepto?

Yes — the fleet is built for delivery duty from day one.

What do I need to sign up?

A driving licence, Aadhaar via DigiLocker, and a phone. Verification takes minutes.
