Why the last mile goes electric first.

It runs on two wheels

Food, groceries and parcels reach India's homes on two-wheelers, all day and late into the night.

Fuel is the rider's biggest cost

An electric scooter turns a daily petrol bill into a predictable daily rent the rider can plan around.

Cities need cleaner air

Delivery scooters drive more kilometres a day than almost anything else on the road. Every one we electrify takes that exhaust off the street.

Energy is the missing piece

A rider paid per order can't stop for hours to charge. Swapping gives them a full battery in minutes.
