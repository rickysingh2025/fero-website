Straight answers

Do I need a down payment or loan?

No. One week's rent plus a refundable deposit. No financing, no EMI, no ownership risk.

What if my scooter breaks down mid-shift?

Bring it to your centre or call for recovery. Repairs are on us — parts at a nominal cost — and you leave on a replacement scooter the same day.

Is the battery swap really free?

Included in your daily rent. (Exact swap policy to be confirmed before launch.)

Can I use it for Zomato, Swiggy, Blinkit or Zepto?

Yes — the fleet is built for delivery duty from day one.

What do I need to sign up?

A driving licence, Aadhaar via DigiLocker, and a phone. Verification takes minutes.

For investors

Investing in Fero Mobility

A rider can't buy a scooter outright. A fleet operator can buy thousands. Fero Mobility owns the fleet and rents it, day by day, to India's delivery riders — energy, maintenance and replacement vehicles bundled into one predictable price. Investors can fund the scooters themselves under a buy-lease structure: you own the asset, Fero runs it.

The model

Utilisation-led revenue

High daily fleet utilisation is the operating target that drives every pricing and centre-density decision.

Wallet health as a margin lever

Share of active riders with a positive wallet balance is tracked as a core metric, not an afterthought.

Fair-wear parts economics

Usage-based part pricing reduces disputes and churn while keeping maintenance margin honest.

No battery on the balance sheet

Battery-less scooters on a swappable-battery network, so the fleet carries no battery capex or degradation risk.

Roadmap

Phase 1 — Gurgaon beachhead

Single-city launch, proving centre density and utilisation before expanding.

Phase 2 — Core metro replication

Rest of Delhi NCR, Bangalore, Mumbai, Hyderabad, Pune and Jaipur — same centre-density playbook, city by city.

Phase 3 — Beyond mobility

Use the rider relationship as a platform for the other things gig workers need — housing and financial services among them.
